FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does PocketVR work without a computer?

Yes. The intended APK installation and normal app use happen on your Android phone. A computer may still be useful for development or debugging, but it is not a normal-use requirement.

Do I need Shizuku?

Not for every feature. Shizuku is only required when a specific feature needs access through an Android service that the app cannot access normally. If a feature does not ask for it, do not install or enable it just because it exists.

Why does my phone get warm?

Camera processing, hand tracking, and rendering can keep the CPU/GPU active. Reduce tracking or visual quality, close background apps, and take a break if the phone becomes uncomfortably hot.

Which phones are compatible?

Compatibility depends on the required sensors, Android version, performance, and viewer fit. Check that your phone has a working rear camera and gyroscope before installing.

Can I update over an existing installation?

If Android refuses to install the new APK over your current copy, uninstall the old version first, then install the new APK. This can happen when the APKs were signed with different keys, such as debug and release builds. Back up any app data you need before uninstalling.
